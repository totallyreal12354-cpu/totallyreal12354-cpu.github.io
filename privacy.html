Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

On This Day Publisher ("the app") is a personal tool operated by its sole user, the owner of the "On This Day" channel. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel, add them to the owner's playlist, and post a comment on the owner's own videos. With the owner's permission through Meta, it publishes the same videos to the owner's Instagram account.

What the app stores

Access tokens and a log of uploaded video IDs are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other person, and it does not read viewers' data.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube access is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
